Why Louisiana. Why now.

The industries that power the country run through Louisiana. The technology that will run them is being built here first.

+

The demand is real.

More than $260B in capital investment has come to Louisiana since 2024, from energy and petrochemicals to shipbuilding and the future of spaceflight. That includes SpaceX’s $100B Starbase Louisiana and Meta’s $50B Hyperion digital factory, each set to be the largest of its kind in the world.

+

The capital is in place.

Louisiana Opportunity Capital, a $100M state venture program, invests through 8 active Louisiana fund partners. The Louisiana Growth Fund writes seed to Series A checks directly into Louisiana startups. Many of those investors are in the room tonight, looking for co-investors.

+

The proving ground is open.

LA.IO and Greater New Orleans, Inc. bring deep connections across the state. We connect startups to what it takes to prove, test and deploy frontier technology from Louisiana: ports and waterways, roads, industrial operators, and the partners and capital behind them.
